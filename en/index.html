Inside the building

What can you discover at PIGGI?

Five ways to get to know Ijen Geopark, all in one place.

  • Geology

    Ijen is more than its blue fire. Learn about the ancient caldera, the acid lake, and the sulfur gas that burns blue.

  • Nature

    From ancient highland fern forests to Ijen's own coffee, the area's plants and animals are introduced here.

  • Culture

    Ijen's landscape grew up alongside its people. Cultural sites and heritage come to life on a projection map.

  • Interactive media

    Immersive video, projection maps, a mini theatre, and guides bring the story to life, not just text on a wall.

  • Hands-on learning

    Geology made fun: watch, observe, and even peek at foraminifera through a microscope.
