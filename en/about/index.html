About PIGGI

One place to understand the story of Ijen.

PIGGI stands for Pusat Informasi Geologi Geopark Ijen, the Ijen Geopark Geological Information Center: an information hub and educational attraction in Banyuwangi.

What is PIGGI?

PIGGI tells the geological story of Ijen Geopark, from how Mount Ijen and its caldera formed to natural wonders like the acid lake and the blue fire. The building is in Banyuwangi and has five floors. [VERIFIKASI]

Beyond geology, PIGGI also introduces Ijen Geopark's plants, animals, and cultural heritage, which is why some people call it a geology museum.

Background

Ijen Geopark spans two regencies, Banyuwangi and Bondowoso. It takes its name from Mount Ijen, the foundation of the area's geological story.

After Ijen Geopark was designated a UNESCO Global Geopark, a single place was needed to bring together everything about the area. Indonesia's Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) supported building the center in recognition of that status. [VERIFIKASI]

A short history

All dates below still need confirmation from PIGGI. [VERIFIKASI]

  1. 24 May 2023

    Ijen Geopark becomes a UNESCO Global Geopark

    This designation sparked the idea of a geological information center in Banyuwangi.

  2. After the designation

    Construction backed by the Geological Agency

    The Ministry of ESDM, through its Geological Agency, supported construction. The building is a former athletes' dormitory on Jalan Gajah Mada, redesigned with a modern look.

  3. 26 January 2024

    Soft launch and public opening

    The Regent of Banyuwangi introduced PIGGI to the public, and it opened as an educational attraction.

  4. 1 July 2024

    Handed over to the Banyuwangi government

    The Geological Agency handed PIGGI over to the Banyuwangi Regency Government at the opening of an international geopark symposium. Since then, the regional government has managed PIGGI.

What PIGGI does

Information hub

Brings everything about Ijen Geopark together in one place. The formation of Mount Ijen, the caldera, the acid lake, and the blue fire are presented step by step through digital media, along with the area's nature and culture.

Educational attraction

Visitors learn hands-on through videos, projection maps, and real geological objects, making earth science easier to grasp for students, families, travelers, and researchers alike.

Our goals

  1. 1

    Understand the land we live on

    The geological history of Banyuwangi and its surroundings is told so everyone can understand it, not just experts.

  2. 2

    Inspire the next generation

    Learning in an engaging, interactive space aims to spark interest in earth science among young people, maybe even future geologists, and to grow earth-science discussion and research in Banyuwangi.

  3. 3

    Strengthen Ijen Geopark

    Explaining to visitors why this globally recognized area matters, and supporting efforts to protect it.

Architecture

A staircase that unfurls like a fern

PIGGI's five-story building has a modern design. Its most striking feature is the staircase: seen from above, it coils into a spiral that resembles an unfurling ancient fern frond.

The shape is inspired by the ancient ferns of the Erek-Erek Geoforest highlands, more than 1,350 m above sea level. It is not just decoration, but a reminder of Ijen Geopark's natural landscape. [VERIFIKASI]
