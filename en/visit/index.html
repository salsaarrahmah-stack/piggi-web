Visitor guidelines

  • Arrive for your booked session and show your booking code at check-in.
  • Take care of the collection and equipment; follow staff instructions at experiment stations.
  • Children must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Cancel your booking if you can't make it, so someone else can use the slot.

Proposed guidelines, pending approval. [KONFIRMASI KE PIGGI]

Privacy notice

Booking details (name, organization, group size, WhatsApp number, email) are used only to manage your visit and send confirmations. They are never sold or shared for other purposes.

Data is stored in systems managed by PIGGI and accessible only to authorized staff. [KONSULTASI UU PDP]

Games at PIGGI never ask for personal data; a nickname is enough.
